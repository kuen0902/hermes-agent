# Japan & Okinawa Travel Strategy

Detailed logistics and booking patterns for maximizing value in Japan.

## 1. Okinawa Hotel Tier Strategy

For a high-end balanced experience, prioritize this "Trinity" pattern:

| Tier | Type | Target Property (Example) | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I (Functional Trend)** | Modern/Urban | **Lequ Okinawa Chatan** | Stay in **Chatan/American Village**. Best for functionality and nightlife. |
| **II (Cultural Zen)** | Luxury Ryotei | **Umi no Ryotei Nakamasou** | Stay in **Onna Village**. Focus on **Half-Board (Kaiseki)**. Essential "Quiet Heart" of the trip. |
| **III (Luxury Fortress)**| Mega Resort | **Ryukyu Hotel & Resort Nashiro Beach** | Stay in **Itoman**. Focus on **Club Lounge**. Best for the "Grand Finale". |

## 2. Booking Platform Optimization (2025-2026)

- **Trip.com**: Best for modern, urban boutique hotels (e.g., Lequ). Often has better "Early Bird" rates than Agoda for these middle-tier properties.
- **Rakuten Travel (Japan Local)**: **Essential for Ryotei/Ryokans**. Ensures you get the correct dinner plans (料理長推薦) which are often missed or incorrectly listed on international OTAs like Booking.com.
- **Agoda / Priceline**: Best for large Luxury Resorts (e.g., Nashiro Beach). Use for **Sunday/Weekday** stays to get 50%+ discounts. Aim for **Club Lounge Access** rooms if the base price drops.

## 3. Logistics: The "No-Car" Problem (North vs South)

### Taichung Airport (RMQ) Access (from Hsinchu/Chupei)
- **Problem**: RMQ is in the North of Taichung; HSR/Taiwan Railway are in the South. 
- **Distance**: ~25km between HSR Taichung and RMQ (~35-45 mins by Taxi).
- **Best Strategy**: **Direct Private Charter (Airport Pick-up)**. Cost is ~1,800-2,000 TWD. More efficient than HSR+Taxi combo which costs ~1,200 TWD but involves 2-3 transfers and 2x time.
- **Bus 156**: Direct from HSR to RMQ, but frequency is too low for flight schedules. Avoid for international departures.

### Okinawa Driving
- **Golden Route**: North (Aquarium) ➔ Middle (Onna) ➔ South (Nashiro/Airport).
- **Pitfall**: Route 58 (Naha to Chatan) has severe rush hour congestion (17:00-19:00). Allow 15-20 min buffer.
