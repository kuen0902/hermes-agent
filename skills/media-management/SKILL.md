# Media Management & Generation

This umbrella skill handles the lifecycle of multimedia content—from consumption and analysis to generation.

## 1. Video Analysis (YouTube)
- Extract transcripts and metadata from YouTube videos.
- Transform transcripts into summaries, X threads, or blog posts.

## 2. Audio & Streaming (Spotify)
- Control playback, search the library, and manage playlists/devices via the Spotify API.
- Search and download GIFs via Tenor and `gif-search`.

## 3. AI Music & Songwriting
- **HeartMuLa**: Suno-like song generation from lyrics and style tags.
- **Craft**: SOPs for songwriting and AI-assisted music production.
- **Analysis**: Use `songsee` for spectrogram and feature extraction (mel, chroma, MFCC).
