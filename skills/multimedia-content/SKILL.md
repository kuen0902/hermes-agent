# Multimedia Generation & Analytics

This umbrella skill captures the full lifecycle of multimedia creation and analysis, from AI-generated visual art and music to video stream processing.

## 1. Visual Art & Graphics
- **Structured Illustration (Baoyu)**: Infographics, comics, and article illustrations.
- **Parametric Workflows**: Advanced node-based automation using ComfyUI.
- **Pixel Art**: Era-specific asset generation for games or retro UI.

## 2. Audio & Music Generation
- **AI Songwriting**: Using HeartMuLa for lyrics-to-song generation.
- **Production Analytics**: Feature extraction (MFCC, Mel) via CLI tools.
- **Streaming Control**: Managing playback and playlists via Spotify API.

## 3. Video Analytics & Transformations
- **Stream Analysis**: Extracting transcripts and metadata from YouTube/MP4.
- **Content Adaptation**: Transforming video transcripts into threads, blogs, or summaries.
- **GIFs**: Search and download workflows for dynamic messaging.
